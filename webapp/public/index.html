meds-signals early signal, not diagnosis

This tool shows statistical early signals from aggregated data. It is not a diagnosis and not medical advice. In an emergency call 123 (Egypt ambulance).

1. Load data

Choose CSV or JSON files. Files are read only inside your browser; nothing is uploaded or stored.

Expected columns and SAMPLE files
  • Pharmacy sales: date, governorate, category, units — SAMPLE_pharmacy_sales.csv
  • Environment: date, governorate, temp_c, pm25, humidity — SAMPLE_environment.csv · SAMPLE_environment.json
  • Workplace: date, governorate, headcount, absences, heat_or_breathing_complaints — SAMPLE_workplace.csv
  • area may replace governorate; optional lat, lon columns place unknown areas on the map. Aggregated counts only — never upload patient or prescription records.

2. Alerts

Method: each series is compared with a robust trailing 28-day baseline (median and MAD) adjusted for day of week. A day is unusual when its robust z-score is ≥ 3; an alert needs 2 consecutive unusual days (or z ≥ 5 once). Environmental readings also alert on fixed thresholds (temperature ≥ 40 °C, PM2.5 ≥ 75 µg/m³). Each unusual day must also be confirmed by a plain-JS Isolation Forest (100 trees, fixed seed) unless z ≥ 5 or a fixed threshold is hit.
